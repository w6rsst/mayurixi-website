♡ About MayuRixi

Whether she's diving into horror games, singing her favorite songs, or creating beautiful art live on stream — Mayu always brings warmth and good vibes to every broadcast. ✧

FAQ

What do you stream? ▾
I stream a variety of content including gaming (horror, cozy games, RPGs), art streams, singing, and chatting sessions! Come hang out~ ♡
What languages do you speak? ▾
I speak Arabic, English, and Japanese! My streams are multilingual and everyone is welcome regardless of language ✧
Can I use your art / clips? ▾
Please ask for permission first before using any of my content. Fan art is always welcome and appreciated! Use the appropriate tags when posting ♡
Do you do commissions? ▾
Commission availability varies! Please check my Twitter for the latest updates on commission status.
How can I support you? ▾
You can support me by following, subscribing on Twitch, donating bits, or simply hanging out in chat! Every bit of support means the world to me 💙
Can I collab with you? ▾
I'm open to collaborations with fellow creators! Please reach out via DM on Twitter or Discord for any collab inquiries ✧
